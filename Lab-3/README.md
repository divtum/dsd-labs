# Lab 3 — Full Adder and Full Subtractor

## Objective

Design and simulate full adder and full subtractor circuits using Verilog HDL.

## Files

- `fa.v` — Full adder implementation.
- `fs.v` — Full subtractor implementation.
- `fa_fs_tb.v` — Testbench for verification.

## Concepts Covered

- Full adder
- Full subtractor
- Carry and borrow generation
- Combinational logic
- Verilog HDL simulation
